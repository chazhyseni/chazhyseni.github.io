# Research

## Overview

My research integrates **machine learning and deep learning approaches** with evolutionary biology to understand how spatial and temporal environmental heterogeneity shapes genetic variation and biological diversity. I specialize in developing computational solutions that bridge AI/ML methods with population genetics, from molecular to community scales.

## Current Research

### AI/ML Applications in De-extinction Science
At Colossal Biosciences, I lead computational efforts to uncover genotype-phenotype associations using:
- **Large language models adapted for DNA sequences** implemented in TensorFlow, Keras, and PyTorch
- Deep learning pipelines for regulatory element prediction and cross-species trait analysis
- Novel algorithms integrating evolutionary theory with neural network architectures
- GPU-optimized computational frameworks for pangenome analysis

## Research Themes

### Machine Learning in Genomics
- Development and training of DNA-specific language models
- Neural network applications for regulatory genomics
- Deep learning for phenotype prediction from genomic data
- Integration of evolutionary constraints in ML model design

### Spatial Connectivity and Optimization
- **Genetic algorithms for resistance surface optimization** in landscape genetics
- Circuit theory-based connectivity modeling for conservation planning
- Omnidirectional connectivity analysis for species movement corridors
- Spatial optimization algorithms for metacommunity dynamics

### Environmental Modeling and Climate Change
- **Physics-informed neural networks** for temperature impact modeling
- **Extreme Learning Machines (ELM)** for climate-biodiversity relationships
- **Random Forest and XGBoost** for species distribution modeling
- Bayesian spatial interpolation with INLA-SPDE for uncertainty quantification

### Evolutionary and Epigenetic Dynamics
- Machine learning approaches to detect signatures of selection
- Epigenetic responses to environmental change using methylation-sensitive techniques
- Integration of genetic and epigenetic data for rapid evolution studies
- Temporal modeling of evolutionary processes under climate change

### Phylogeography and Historical Biogeography
- Bayesian inference for phylogenetic reconstruction
- Spatial modeling of historical demographic processes
- Machine learning for biogeographic pattern detection
- Integration of paleoclimatic data with genetic patterns

### Urban Ecology and Conservation
Investigating how urbanization affects biodiversity at genetic and community levels:
- Landscape connectivity in urban environments
- Metapopulation dynamics and gene flow
- Urban pond ecosystems and aquatic biodiversity
- Environmental DNA applications for urban biodiversity monitoring
